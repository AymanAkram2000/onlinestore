# Modest Fashion & Hijab Store (متجر أزياء المحجبات والمنقبات)

The core domain for the modest fashion, abaya, niqab, hijab scarves, and accessories e-commerce platform, featuring color-linked galleries, modest apparel sizing, stock availability states, guest checkout with WhatsApp confirmation, and store owner administrative controls.

## Language

**Product**:
A distinct modest merchandise item (e.g., Maxi Dress, Gulf Abaya, Malaysian Khimar, Chiffon Tarha, Magnetic Pins) containing base details such as title, description, category, and base price.
_Avoid_: Item, goods, listing

**Color Variant**:
A specific color shade of a garment or scarf (e.g., Royal Black, Dusty Rose, Mint Green, Sand Beige), owning its dedicated photo gallery, hex color code, and independent availability switch (Active/Inactive).
_Avoid_: Shade option, picture group

**Size Option**:
A sizing specification adapted for modest fashion (e.g., Abaya lengths 52/54/56/58/60, Dress sizes 1/2/3 or S/M/L/XL, Free Size for Khimar, or 'No Size' for scarves and magnetic accessories).
_Avoid_: Dimension, weight, cut

**Shipping Zone**:
A geographic area (Egyptian governorates) paired with a delivery fee and transit timeframe.
_Avoid_: Shipping region, district

**Category**:
A classification tier for modest fashion (Abayas & Dresses, Niqabs & Khimars, Scarves & Shawls, Hijab Accessories).
_Avoid_: Department, section

**Cart**:
A temporary holding container of chosen modest wear items and quantities assembled by the customer before checkout.
_Avoid_: Basket, shopping bag

**Order**:
A finalized purchase agreement containing customer contact/shipping details, itemized variant selections, total price, and fulfillment status.
_Avoid_: Transaction, deal, checkout invoice

**Customer**:
A shopper purchasing modest apparel, either via streamlined guest checkout or an authenticated profile.
_Avoid_: Buyer, visitor, account

**Marketing Badge**:
A visual flag on a product card highlighting special merchandising status (e.g., 'وصل حديثاً', 'الأكثر طلباً', 'طقم مميز').
_Avoid_: Ribbon, sticker, label tag

**Size Guide**:
A measurement reference chart tailored for abayas and dresses (height, chest width, length in cm) to assist modest wear shoppers.
_Avoid_: Fit manual, size chart table

**Store Owner**:
The authenticated business administrator responsible for managing the product catalog, updating color availability, and overseeing orders.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator, supervisor
